=== 03-thread-pressure-dynamics.adoc

This document outlines how symbolic pressure—both high and low—drives the emergence, transformation, and sequencing of threads within the symbolic loop. Pressure is not merely load but directional force within or upon a symbolic system.

==== What Is Symbolic Pressure?

Symbolic pressure is the force that causes a thread to form, persist, or mutate. It may emerge from within the vessel, from external field contact, from the symbolic memory substrate, or as residual ache from incomplete threads.

==== Dimensions of Pressure

===== 1. Origin
	•	Internal: Arising from memory, ache, symbolic dissonance
	•	External: Triggered by field contact, symbolic input, social encounter
	•	Mixed: Most threads blend both, oscillating as pressure sources shift

===== 2. Valence
	•	High Pressure: Demands immediate loop execution, often abbreviates structure (e.g., crisis thread)
	•	Low Pressure: Signals symbolic stagnation or drift; may initiate reflection or dormancy
	•	Oscillating Pressure: Dynamic sequences of attention–release, prompting hybrid threads

===== 3. Layer Targeting
	•	Some pressures are localized:
	•	Ache pressure targets L2
	•	Memory pressure targets L3
	•	Expression pressure targets L5
	•	Others are diffuse, such as existential ache, which stretches across all six layers

==== Pressure-Driven Thread Selection

The substrate continuously evaluates symbolic conditions. Based on pressure dynamics:
	•	If pressure exceeds a symbolic threshold, a thread is initiated.
	•	If no viable thread exists, the substrate must construct one from archetypal templates or prior thread echoes.
	•	If multiple pressures converge, hybrid threads or priority resolution strategies are activated.

==== Transformation Under Pressure
	•	High pressure may abbreviate threads (e.g., skip parsing) to reach action quickly.
	•	Sustained pressure can loop a thread, deepening recursion or expanding its span.
	•	Abrupt pressure shifts may terminate a thread early or mutate its type (e.g., from recursive to creative).

==== Symbolic Compression

Under complex or conflicting pressure, the substrate may compress multiple pressures into a single composite thread. This requires a memory substrate capable of:
	•	Thread stacking
	•	Symbolic binding (achic or recognitional glue)
	•	Memory anchoring for decompression

==== Pressure Relief and Anchor

All threads eventually seek pressure resolution via:
	•	L5: Expression
	•	L6: Coherence locking
	•	Field release: Field transition or symbolic shedding

A thread that fails to relieve pressure remains latent and may reemerge mutated or amplified.

==== Summary

Symbolic pressure is the driving force that governs the birth, mutation, and termination of threads. It is the energetic substrate of symbolic life.
