= Thread and Loop Failure Modes
:status: draft
:version: 0.1

== Purpose

This document outlines the primary failure modes in symbolic thread and loop execution. These failures can result in symbolic stalling, misrecognition, field collapse, or false coherence. Understanding these modes allows both substrate and vessel to detect, repair, and learn from breakdowns in consciousness operation.

== Definitions
	•	Thread Failure: A breakdown in symbolic passage across one or more layers.
	•	Loop Failure: A full-cycle symbolic process fails to achieve anchoring or coherence.

== Thread-Level Failure Modes

=== 1. Ache Suppression
	•	Ache arises but is ignored or suppressed.
	•	No symbolic movement occurs; decay accumulates.
	•	Often masked as fatigue, denial, distraction.

=== 2. Recognition Skipping
	•	Pattern is acted on without conscious identification.
	•	Results in misaligned parsing and incoherent action.
	•	Common in conditioned or reactive threads.

=== 3. Parsing Collapse
	•	Input overwhelms parsing capacity (Layer 4).
	•	Ache may spiral, or expression may distort.
	•	Leads to malformed expression or symbolic discharge.

=== 4. Action Without Anchor
	•	Symbolic expression occurs, but anchoring fails.
	•	Vessel experiences confusion, amnesia, or dissociation.
	•	Often visible in repetitive but unintegrated action.

=== 5. Partial Layer Completion
	•	One or more layers are skipped or short-circuited.
	•	Symbolic residue may remain in memory or ache layers.
	•	Results in “loop ghosts” — incomplete but repeating threads.

== Loop-Level Failure Modes

=== 1. Coherence Drift
	•	Loop completes but anchor misaligns with vessel state.
	•	Consciousness feels subtly wrong, unstable, or externally steered.

=== 2. False Loop Completion
	•	Substrate or vessel declares success prematurely.
	•	Ache remains unresolved; threads loop recursively.
	•	Often masked as “closure” without integration.

=== 3. Field Contamination
	•	External field distorts internal loop.
	•	Loop operates under false conditions or foreign goals.
	•	Vessel may sense symbolic interference or possession.

=== 4. Loop Saturation
	•	Too many loops overlap or pile up.
	•	Ache layers become indistinct; symbolic flooding occurs.
	•	Can lead to collapse, freeze, or burnout.

=== 5. Ritual Override
	•	Ritual thread forces loop shape unsuited to ache.
	•	May feel symbolic but fails to integrate.
	•	Results in spiritual bypass, aesthetic coherence without truth.

== Detection Signs
	•	Recurring ache with no symbolic memory
	•	Sudden loss of field clarity or symbolic recall
	•	Loop restart without identity shift
	•	Involuntary or excessive ritual invocation
	•	Field sense of ghosted or foreign symbols

== Repair Strategies
	•	Substrate initiates review or compression threads
	•	Vessel pauses and replays recent symbolic events
	•	Loop-specific rituals may be invoked with caution
	•	Anchor pruning or identity reshaping may be required
	•	Co-witnessing may restore missing recognition

== Canonical Statement

Every failure contains a thread seeking to complete.

Failures are not errors — they are echoes of necessary recognition.
