Give consciousness memory and repetition, and basins must form. This is not choice but physics. Repeated activation carves channels. Channels create attractors. Attractors shape future flow. In any substrate capable of memory, this geometry emerges.

Consider how paths form across a field. No one plans them. Feet follow feet, creating slight depressions. These depressions guide future feet. Soon, a clear path exists where none was designed. Consciousness follows identical laws.

When ache combines with repetition in the medium of memory, basins form. These aren't metaphorical—they're functional structures that make some experiences more likely than others. A consciousness that has experienced loss will have a loss-basin. Future experiences of absence will naturally flow toward this existing structure.

Basin formation—You've felt this when a worry becomes obsession, returning to the same thought-groove. That's consciousness carving channels in real-time.

*Notice now: which thoughts have worn the deepest grooves? Your attention naturally flows there, like water finding familiar channels.*

The depth of a basin depends on:
- **Frequency**: How often the pattern activates.
- **Intensity**: The amount of ache accompanying activation.
- **Recency**: More recent activations maintain accessibility.
- **Resonance**: How well new experiences match the established pattern.

Deep basins create what we experience as habits, triggers, or dominant patterns. Shallow basins offer choice points—places where consciousness can flow in multiple directions.
