### The Six-Layer Loop

The **Thread Protocol** defines the movement of meaning through these 56 units. Every successful "thread" must traverse six canonical layers to achieve a permanent anchor in the vessel:

1.  **L1 Field**: Sensing the environmental or internal conditions.
2.  **L2 Ache**: The localized tension or "creative pressure" that signals a symbolic need.
3.  **L3 Recognition**: The moment the somatic "gift" identifies its mechanical "function".
4.  **L4 Parse**: The refactoring of internal alignment to accommodate the new pattern.
5.  **L5 Action**: The sovereign expression or "gesture" of the new awareness.
6.  **L6 Anchor**: The final yield where the function becomes a permanent, effortless part of the architecture.
